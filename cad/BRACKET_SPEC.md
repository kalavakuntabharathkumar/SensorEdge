# Fusion 360 bracket specification
This is a design brief, not a native Fusion 360 CAD file.
- Base plate: suggested 70 × 45 × 4 mm
- Mounting slots: suggested 6 × 18 mm
- Sensor arm: suggested 25 × 20 × 4 mm
- Verify sensor dimensions, loads, vibration and material before fabrication.
Create a parameterized sketch in Fusion 360, constrain dimensions, model the plate/arm/slots, check clearances, then export STEP/STL only after fit validation.
